# Induction Motor Maintenance Manual (MM-MTR-021)

## 1. Winding Temperature

Class F insulation, class B temperature rise. Alarm 130 °C, trip 145 °C (RTD). Causes of high winding temperature: overload of the driven equipment, blocked cooling air path or failed cooling fan, high ambient temperature, voltage unbalance above 1 %, frequent starting.

## 2. High Motor Current

Sustained current above full-load current indicates mechanical overload (binding pump, closed discharge throttling, high fluid density) or supply problems. Check driven equipment and supply voltage before increasing protection settings.

## 3. Starting Limits

Medium-voltage motors are limited to two consecutive cold starts or one hot start, followed by a 30-minute cooling period. Exceeding starting limits overheats the rotor cage.

## 4. Insulation Testing

For 6.6 kV motors, insulation resistance at 5 kV DC shall exceed 100 MΩ (corrected to 40 °C) and the polarization index shall exceed 2.0. Perform after any earth fault trip, flooding, or prolonged standstill in humid conditions.

## 5. Bearings and Vibration

Re-grease anti-friction bearings according to the nameplate interval. Vibration alert 4.5 mm/s. Check coupling alignment and foundation bolts when vibration rises.
