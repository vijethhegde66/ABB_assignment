# General Process Safety Instructions (SAF-GEN-040)

## 1. Protective Systems

1.1 Trips, interlocks and safety instrumented functions shall never be bypassed, inhibited or forced without a bypass permit approved by the shift supervisor and, for SIL-rated functions, the area authority. Alarms shall not be disabled to stop nuisance annunciation; use the rationalisation process (ALM-PHIL-030).

1.2 Any person may use Stop Work Authority when conditions are unsafe.

## 2. Permit to Work

Line breaking, hot work, confined space entry and electrical isolation require a valid permit to work. Field operators shall check gas detection before entering process areas after a release alarm.

## 3. Fired Heaters

3.1 On Heater Outlet Temperature High, reduce the firing rate and verify charge flow; low charge flow (e.g. after a charge pump trip) rapidly overheats heater tubes.

3.2 On Heater Flame Failure the burner management system trips the fuel supply. Do not attempt relight before the furnace has been purged for the required purge time.

## 4. Hydrotreater Reactors

On Reactor Bed Temperature High, reduce heater outlet temperature and increase quench gas. A bed temperature rising faster than 5 °C per minute requires emergency depressurisation according to the unit emergency procedure.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment

Minimum PPE in process areas: hard hat, safety glasses, flame-resistant clothing, safety shoes, gloves and hearing protection in high-noise areas (pumps, compressors).
